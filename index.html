1 · Contacts added to sequences

Contacts newly enrolled into a sequence, counted on their addingDate. One source row = one contact-in-one-sequence, so the same person enrolled in 3 sequences counts 3 times unless Unique contacts only is ticked. Owner is the first name on the sequence (Rahul_US_Direct_July → Rahul), since licence seats are shared between people.
